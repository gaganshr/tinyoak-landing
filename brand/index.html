The idea

Why an oak

The oak is the oldest metaphor for earned growth: nothing forces it, and nothing stops it. Tiny Oak takes performance management from an administrative ritual to an organisational capability — the same way a tiny oak becomes a mighty one.

A review is not an event. It's a thread your team runs through the year. Goals set, progress logged, a conversation had — small, repeated acts that most tools make forgettable and Tiny Oak makes count.

Every mighty oak was once an acorn that held its ground. Tiny Oak is named for the beginning of that story, not the end: the small thing, tended on a rhythm, becoming something that leads.

01 — Small over sudden. We reward the habit, not the heroics. Consistency is the product.

02 — A thread, not an event. One cycle, three movements, continuous in between.

03 — Rooted, not rigid. Grounded and calm, never bureaucratic. Reviews that build leaders, not paperwork.

The framework

LIGHT: a leadership ladder built on service.

Tiny Oak organises leadership growth around five levels, each defined by who it serves — from keeping the business alive to serving a higher purpose. Every goal maps to a rung, and each rung carries its own colour.

Voice & tone

Plain-spoken and grounded

Write like a good manager talks: clear, warm, and specific. We name things by what people recognise, use active voice, and never dress up a simple idea. British spelling throughout.

Grounded

Concrete over clever. We'd rather be useful than impressive.

YesYou're 2 days from the weekly cut-off.
NotTime is running out — act now!

Encouraging

We're on the team's side. Progress is noticed; a miss is a nudge, not a verdict.

YesNice — three weeks logged in a row.
NotYou have finally maintained compliance.

Precise

Say the number, the date, the next step. Vagueness is the enemy of trust.

YesLog progress on 2 goals by Fri.
NotSome items may require your attention.
