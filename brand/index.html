The idea

Why an oak

The oak is the oldest metaphor for earned growth: nothing forces it, and nothing stops it. That is how real performance is built — not in a sprint, but in a cadence.

Great teams aren't built in bursts. They're built in small, repeated acts — a goal set, an update logged, a bit of feedback given — that most tools make forgettable and Tiny Oak makes count.

Every mighty oak was once an acorn that held its ground. Tiny Oak is named for the beginning of that story, not the end: the small thing, tended well, on a rhythm, becoming something that lasts.

01 — Small over sudden. We reward the habit, not the heroics. Consistency is the product.

02 — Cadence over chaos. A predictable weekly rhythm beats an annual scramble.

03 — Rooted, not rigid. Grounded and calm, never bureaucratic. The tool gets out of the way.

The mark

An acorn: the whole promise in one glyph. Use the horizontal lockup as the default; the mark alone where space is tight or the brand is already established.

Mark, reversed on brand
DoGive the mark clear space equal to the height of the acorn cap. Keep it a single solid colour: brand purple, ink, or white.
DoUse the mark alone once the wordmark has been seen — in avatars, favicons, and app chrome.
Don'tRecolour with gradients, add shadows to the flat mark, or stretch the proportions.

Voice & tone

Plain-spoken and grounded

Write like a good manager talks: clear, warm, and specific. We name things by what people recognise, use active voice, and never dress up a simple idea.

Grounded

Concrete over clever. We'd rather be useful than impressive.

YesYou're 2 days from the weekly cut-off.
NotTime is running out — act now!

Encouraging

We're on the team's side. Progress is noticed; misses are a nudge, not a verdict.

YesNice — three weeks logged in a row.
NotYou have finally maintained compliance.

Precise

Say the number, the date, the next step. Vagueness is the enemy of trust.

YesLog progress on 2 goals by Fri.
NotSome items may require your attention.
